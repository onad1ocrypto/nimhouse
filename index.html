NimHouse — seven skill-based mini games inside Nimiq Pay

NimHouse is a free mini app for Nimiq Pay containing seven skill-based games. Every score is earned: there are no entry fees, no betting and no chance-based outcomes. Cosmetic items can be bought with NIM, and they never affect gameplay.

NimChick

Flap an absurd mustached chicken through endless pipes. Classic flapper, zero mercy. Tap to flap.

NimStack

Stack blocks with pixel-perfect precision. Every overhang gets sliced off. Tap to drop.

NimBullseye

Classic dartboard with BULL 50, T and D rings. A chicken crosses the board — land on it and lose 10. Tap to aim, hold and release.

NimRush

Pseudo-3D road, full speed, zero brakes. Weave through cows, UFOs and giant sausages. Tap left or right.

NimSwat

One angry fly with a mustache. Tap it before it gets too fast. Five misses and it escapes.

NimSlice

Sausage volleys incoming — swipe to slice them all, never the black one.

NimDash

Sprint, jump and mid-air flap over blocks, pits and mustachioed flies. Stomp flies, grab Luna coins.
